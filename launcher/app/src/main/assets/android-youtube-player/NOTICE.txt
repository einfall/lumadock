Luma Dock uses the published Maven Central library for visible YouTube playback.
The library uses YouTube's iframe player. Player controls are disabled with
IFramePlayerOptions.Builder(context).controls(0); preview controls are native Luma Dock buttons.

For the visible Home background, Luma Dock adds its own cosmetic stylesheet to the
YouTube frame before rendering through AndroidX WebKit 1.10.0 (Apache 2.0).
Title/chrome and playback feedback are hidden outside advertising playback.
The video, ads, captions and player errors are not modified. This optional styling
requires DOCUMENT_START_SCRIPT support and depends on YouTube's DOM structure.
